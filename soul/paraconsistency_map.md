## META-INSIGHT
Heat pairs share common terms with bridge-possible relation types. Silence pairs share terms BUT their relation types have no bridge (forbids vs sometimes-requires, builds vs requires). Some value tensions live in the META-structure of priorities, not in the content of propositions. NAL reads propositions. The hierarchy reads the space between them. Both are reasoning systems. Neither alone is sufficient.

## Connection to Soul Gate Roadmap
Silences are exactly where the static floor should live - cases where formal inference finds no bridge, meaning no relaxation should occur. Heat signatures are where the learning band can safely operate - both directions contain truth, context can determine which dominates.
