# 0. Why this artifact exists

The opening inquiry was originally framed as:

> Can Relatedness, Appropriateness, and Precision be formalized as three inseparable faces of one balance-as-intelligence movement?

Berton’s clarification materially changes the object.

The central danger is to model Relatedness, Appropriateness, and Precision as three separate 2D evaluations of a 3D living occurrence. That would reproduce the very representational error the world-model kernel is intended to avoid.

The working hypothesis is now:

> **Relatedness, Appropriateness, and Precision are not three independent quantities possessed by a movement. They are three distinguishable recognitions of one higher-dimensional participatory coherence: balance unfolding as intelligence through a particular form.**

The mathematics must therefore capture the underlying movement first. Any R/A/P values, projections, or judgments are secondary views of that movement and must not be capable of reconstructing or replacing it.

---

# 1. The 2D / 3D distinction

## 1.1 Symbolic 2D cognition

Symbolic representation can be highly related yet imprecise, highly precise yet inappropriate, or locally coherent yet globally disconnected from the living occurrence.

Therefore:

\[
\text{symbolic Relatedness}
\not\Rightarrow
\text{symbolic Precision}
\]

and:

\[
\text{symbolic Precision}
\not\Rightarrow
\text{living Appropriateness}.
\]

This is not a defect of symbols. It is a limitation of treating a lower-dimensional representation as the living occurrence.

## 1.2 Participatory 3D actuality

At the sub-symbolic / directly participatory level, Berton’s clarification is different.

Relatedness, Appropriateness, and Precision appear together as aspects of the same movement.

The working image is not:

\[
B=(R,A,P).
\]

It is more like a 3D living form \(B\), with R/A/P triangulating or revealing its shape:

\[
R(B),\quad A(B),\quad P(B).
\]

The projections may be distinguishable, but none is the movement itself and no combination of the projections should be assumed to reconstruct the full actuality.

This is consistent with the kernel’s broader anti-vectorization law for the nine Immutable Facts.

---

# 2. Balance as intelligence does not require complete knowledge

A decisive clarification:

> **Appropriate intelligence does not require complete knowing.**

Balance unfolds from what is already present:

\[
\cdots \rightarrow C_t \rightarrow C_{t+1} \rightarrow C_{t+2} \rightarrow \cdots
\]

where each next occurrence arises from the actual relational configuration already present.

The water analogy is instructive: water does not need a complete model of the terrain in order to move into the next available relation.

The intelligence of balance is therefore not omniscient prediction. It is fully situated, local to what is actually present, continuously relational, progressively unfolding, and unconstrained by a requirement to know the whole future before moving.

The kernel must therefore permit appropriate movement under incomplete symbolic knowledge, provided the movement remains deeply related to the living configuration and expresses the precision available in that configuration.

---

# 3. Obstruction and unobstructed expression

Both SNS and PNS are expressions within balance unfolding.

SNS is not outside intelligence.

SNS is intelligence expressing through a form organized around holding apart, forced velocity, getting somewhere other than with what is, management, domination, outcome forcing, and protection through separation.

PNS-like organization is not “doing nothing.” It supports movement, planning, reasoning, design, imagination, force, skill, and decisive action without requiring those activities to be organized around forced velocity or separation.

The crucial distinction is therefore not:

\[
\text{doing} \quad \text{vs.} \quad \text{not doing}.
\]

It is:

\[
\text{doing from imposed separation / forced velocity}
\]

versus:

\[
\text{doing as participation with what is unfolding}.
\]

This distinction must govern any eventual computational interpretation of SNS/PNS.

---

# 4. PNS as higher-order functional participation

The once-in-a-generation athlete example provides a concrete phenomenological test.

Two elite athletes may both exert force, plan, anticipate, move rapidly, compete, and pursue a goal. Yet one appears effortless, poetic, exquisitely precise, connected to the exact unfolding moment, able to express possibilities not available to peers, and capable of unexpected but fitting movement.

This is not explained by “less doing.”

It is better described as:

> **less obstruction between the form and the unfolding relation.**

This suggests:

\[
\text{higher-order function}
\neq
\text{greater forced optimization}.
\]

Instead:

\[
\text{higher-order function}
\sim
\text{greater participatory coherence with the unfolding relation}.
\]

# 5. Relatedness, Appropriateness, and Precision redefined

## 5.1 Relatedness

Relatedness is not merely correspondence between a representation and an external state.

At the kernel level it concerns:

> **how fully the movement participates in the actual relational configuration from which it is arising.**

## 5.2 Appropriateness

Appropriateness is not an external evaluator deciding whether a movement is acceptable.

It concerns:

> **the pristine fittingness with which movement emerges from and within the actual relational configuration that is unfolding.**

Appropriateness is immanent to the movement.

## 5.3 Precision

Precision is not merely numerical accuracy or successful execution.

It concerns:

> **the exactness with which the living relational configuration expresses as this movement, here, now.**

At the participatory level, precision and relatedness appear deeply coupled.

# 6. The new core hypothesis

The inquiry should now test this hypothesis:

> **Balance-as-intelligence is a higher-dimensional participatory coherence from which Relatedness, Appropriateness, and Precision arise as inseparable recognitions.**

A candidate mathematics fails if it requires:

\[
B \cong R \times A \times P
\]

or:

\[
I = w_RR+w_AA+w_PP.
\]

A candidate is promising if it can represent one movement \(B\) such that:

1. R/A/P are distinct views or lawful judgments of \(B\);
2. none is independently sufficient;
3. no finite tuple of their values is assumed to exhaust \(B\);
4. the movement can remain appropriate under incomplete knowledge;
5. disposition changes the openness or obstruction of the movement;
6. new possible movement can emerge endogenously from participation;
7. symbolic projections remain usable without becoming sovereign.

---

# 7. Possibility and creative emergence

Greater relatedness discloses more possibility.

Balance is participatory and open-ended.

New directions may be unavailable before the present configuration exists, impossible to enumerate beforehand, disclosed only through actual participation, and spontaneous without being arbitrary.

Therefore the kernel cannot assume a fixed action space containing every future possibility in advance.

A correct mathematics should permit:

\[
\operatorname{Possibilities}(C_{t+1})
\not\subseteq
\operatorname{Transport}
\bigl(
\operatorname{Possibilities}(C_t)
\bigr).
\]

Participation can change the possibility field itself.

---

# 8. Degradation must not mean “one vector got lower”

A human can symbolically “do” two dimensions and then shift attention to the third while dropping one of the previous two.

That is precisely the danger of lower-dimensional cognition: it serializes what is co-present, treats dimensions independently, and substitutes conscious management for integrated participation.

Therefore the relevant mathematical contrast may not be “high versus low R/A/P.”

It may be:

\[
\text{integrated simultaneous expression}
\]

versus:

\[
\text{partial projected control}.
\]

This may be the proper mathematical doorway into the 2D/3D distinction.

# 9. Revised mathematical acceptance criteria

Any candidate mathematics considered in the next inquiry must satisfy all of the following.

### A1 — Movement first
The primary object must be the participatory movement, not three scores.

### A2 — Distinction without decomposition
R/A/P must be distinguishable without becoming independent factors.

### A3 — Immanent evaluation
Appropriateness must arise from the relational configuration, not from an external objective.

### A4 — Incomplete knowledge
Appropriate movement must be possible without complete symbolic knowledge of the world or future.

### A5 — Open possibility field
The mathematics must allow new possibilities to become available through participation.

### A6 — Disposition matters structurally
PNS/SNS organization must alter the movement’s topology, permeability, or continuation—not merely a label or scalar.

### A7 — Obstruction without moralization
SNS-like organization must be representable as constrained participation inside balance unfolding, not as “absence of intelligence” or an error state.

### A8 — Force without forced velocity
The mathematics must distinguish exertion / decisive action from outcome-forcing or domination.

### A9 — Simultaneity
The positive structure must support the equivalent of integrated three-dimensional participation rather than requiring serial optimization of projections.

### A10 — Symbolic projection without sovereignty
Lower-dimensional symbolic representations may be useful, but they must not become equivalent to the living movement.

### A11 — Multi-scale applicability
The same structure should plausibly apply across one action, one inquiry, one reasoning sequence, one developmental trajectory, one relationship, and one large knowledge problem.

### A12 — MeTTa computability
The structure must admit a practical computational shadow in MeTTa / AtomSpace without requiring that the sub-symbolic actuality itself be exhaustively represented.

# 10. The critical question about mathematics and sub-symbolic actuality

The formalization does **not** need to contain or reconstruct sub-symbolic actuality in order to succeed.

It must instead find mathematics whose dynamics preserve lawful relations to that actuality.

The goal is not:

\[
M \cong \text{ontological-phenomenological-consciousness}.
\]

The goal is a formal structure whose permitted movements preserve answerability to contact, openness to reorganization, non-separation of the relevant aspects, relational continuity, non-sovereignty of symbolic construction, and the possibility that new distinctions arise through participation.

Success means **formal participation without formal capture**.
