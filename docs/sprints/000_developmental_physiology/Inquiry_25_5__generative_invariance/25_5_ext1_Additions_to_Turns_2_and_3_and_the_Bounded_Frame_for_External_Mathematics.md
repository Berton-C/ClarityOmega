# Part II. Additions to 25.5c and the bounded frame for external mathematics

## II-1. Invert the frame: from family-first to role-first, with a saturation stop rule

25.5c's own best result is Section 9: the discovery, after the fact, that the five families decompose into four roles. The reframing is to make that decomposition the **method**, derived from the acceptance surface before any family is examined, so that coverage and boundedness are both provable properties rather than hopes.

**Step 1: derive the role partition from A1 to A20.** The acceptance surface itself forces exactly this decomposition:

- **Role H (open host):** forced by A2, A3, A4, A5 (carriers, interfaces, relational organization, locality).
- **Role U (unfolding law and its equivalence):** forced by A1, A14, A18 (one engine, consequence, structural equivalence).
- **Role C (accessibility and disposition):** forced by A7, A8, A9, A10 (gradient, engine-preserving obstruction, reflexivity, disclosure).
- **Role R (recognition):** forced by A13, A17, A19 (non-reconstruction, Immutable-Fact unity, non-sovereignty).
- **Cross-cutting obligations that are not roles:** A6 and A12 (no global objective; R/A/P necessity). These are constraints on how the roles combine, not slots for machinery, and treating them as import targets is precisely how rabbit holes start.

**Step 2: the stop rule.**

> **Role-saturation stop rule.** Turn 3 is complete when (i) every forced role has at least one adequate candidate, (ii) every retained candidate is load-bearing for exactly one role (typed, non-overlapping), and (iii) every examined-and-rejected candidate has a recorded elimination test and a recorded re-admission trigger. Turn 3 is NOT complete when some count of families has been reviewed, and it is NOT incomplete merely because mathematics exists that was never examined.

Clause (iii) is what answers the "not looking deeply can really cost us" worry without unbounding the survey: nothing is excluded by silence. Everything excluded is excluded by a named test, and everything excluded carries a named condition under which it must be reconsidered. The cost of missing something collapses from "unknowable" to "the price of one wrong elimination test," which is auditable.

**Step 3: candidate admission discipline per role.** A candidate may be examined only against the role that forces it, with the role's own A-criteria as the test battery. Cross-role ambitions (a family claiming two roles) are the signature of a sovereignty risk and require explicit justification. This one rule would have flagged E4's danger (an energy formalism reaching for both U and C) before any analysis was spent on it.

Under this frame, 25.5c's existing work is not discarded: it is re-founded. Its five families and its Section 9 roles survive; what changes is that the boundedness argument becomes principled, two candidates reclassify (II-2), one role turns out to have an internal primary candidate (II-3), and the missing coherence layer becomes visible (II-5).

## II-5. The missing layer in 25.5c: role-interface coherence obligations

Section 10 correctly warns that four roles side by side are eclecticism unless integrated around one law, but it does not state the integration conditions. They should be fixed now, because they are the actual hard part of Turn 4, and discovering them mid-construction is how Turn 4 sprawls. Four coherence laws:

1. **Equivalence respects composition (U with H).** BehEq of composite open systems is derivable from BehEq of components along matched interfaces: the lax squares compose horizontally in the equipment. Without this, "one engine" fragments at every interface.
2. **Constraint respects equivalence (C with U).** If two carriers are equivalent and their constraints correspond under the carrier relation, the constrained systems are equivalent to at least the composed grade. Without this, disposition differences could silently break the invariance claim, and SNS would falsify one-engine by construction.
3. **Recognition respects equivalence (R with U).** Recognitions cannot distinguish behaviorally equivalent unfoldings more finely than the equivalence grade permits. Without this, the recognition family becomes sovereign over the movement, violating B10 and A19 in one move.
4. **One clock (all roles with proto-time).** Host composition, unfolding, constraint change, and recognition are indexed by the same inherited proto-time cuts, with the guard discipline applying to any claim about constraint release or reorganization. Without this, the roles drift onto private clocks and B7 becomes unprovable.

Each law is also the falsifier of a specific integration failure, so they double as the Turn-4 mutation set.

## II-6. A candidate closure for G5: creation and degradation as boundary-relative readings of one open law

The one-law demand for creation and degradation may not need new mathematics at all once the host is genuinely open:

> **Boundary-relative reading (candidate lemma).** In an open system, "degradation" is unfolding whose continuation lands increasingly outside the carrier's boundary (flow through the interface away from the form's organization), and "creation" is unfolding whose continuation lands increasingly inside newly composed relational structure. The law G is the same term in both; the difference is where, relative to the carrier boundary, the continuation is carried. Closed-system decay is open-system flow.

This uses only Role H machinery already retained, is neutral between preferred and degrading outcomes (F2, F23 safe), and gives the C10 control a precise shape: a dissipating manifestation where the same G, the same equivalence, and the same recognitions remain non-decorative while the boundary-relative direction reverses. If this lemma survives, G5 closes without any import beyond E1, and E4's manifestation role becomes its permanent status.
