# 8. Existing structure F — approximate morphisms and self-continuity

**Classification: BRIDGEABLE — HIGHEST VALUE FOR `StructuralEquiv`**

Hyperseed Definition 216 defines degree of a structure-preserving map between V-weighted graphs, and Theorem 14 gives a composition law for approximate morphisms.

Section 16 then uses such mappings to model self-continuity across time without demanding token identity.

This is the strongest existing internal mathematical precedent for the Turn-1 target:

\[
T_{ij}\circ\mathcal G_{C_i}
\simeq
\mathcal G_{C_j}\circ T_{ij}.
\]

## What approximate morphisms provide

They already let us express:

- source and target carriers are not identical;
- structure can be preserved approximately;
- preservation composes;
- long chains can carry controlled degradation;
- structural continuity is distinct from numerical similarity.

This is highly aligned with:

- **A2** carrier-independence;
- **A3** form-specific expression;
- **A18** structural equivalence;
- **F16** equivalence must not mean carrier identity.

## Critical limitation

Approximate morphism degree currently compares **represented relational structures**.

It does not by itself define a commuting law for **generative dynamics**.

In particular, current Hyperseed gives something like:

\[
f : G \rightsquigarrow H
\]

with a preservation degree.

25.5 needs something stronger:

\[
f\circ \mathcal G_G
\;\simeq\;
\mathcal G_H\circ f
\]

or an equivalent relation between transformation processes.

## Turn-2 ruling

This is a major internal foundation for StructuralEquiv, but the **dynamic commuting condition remains a named gap**.

# 12. Existing structure J — Wu Wei / minimal forcing

\[

unless such an identification is independently earned.

The baseline “passive dynamics” can also become representation-sovereign if treated as actuality rather than a model.

## Turn-2 ruling

Wu Wei is **not enough to close A7**.

It identifies the right phenomenon but does not yet provide the right kernel-level semantics.

This is a named gap for Turn 3.
