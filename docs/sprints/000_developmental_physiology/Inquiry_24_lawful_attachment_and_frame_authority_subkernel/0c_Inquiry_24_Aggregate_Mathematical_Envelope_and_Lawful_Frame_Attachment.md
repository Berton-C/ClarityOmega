6. **Further mathematical literature and external analyses.**  
   They are admitted only when they close a named obligation or resolve a named proof gap.

## 1.2 Status vocabulary

This artifact uses the following statuses:

- **GOVERNING:** established by `006` or inherited ClarityOmega canon.
- **ACCEPTED DERIVATION:** follows from the governing ground closely enough to direct formalization.
- **PROVISIONAL CONSTRUCTION:** a mathematically serious candidate that must be tested.
- **DEFERRED:** potentially valuable but not yet sufficiently specified or necessary.
- **REJECTED AS STATED:** contains a useful direction but cannot enter in its proposed form.
- **OPEN:** a genuine remaining mathematical obligation.

## 1.3 Anti-rabbit-hole rule

No new mathematical family should enter the survey merely because it is elegant or adjacent.

A new family may enter only if it does at least one of the following:

1. supplies a missing construction required by a named obligation;
2. proves a coherence result for the current envelope;
3. supplies a counterexample showing that a current candidate cannot work;
4. materially simplifies the envelope without losing an obligation;
5. yields a finite computational projection or falsifier unavailable in the current envelope.

This is the formal stop-rule against an endless survey.

# 3. The mathematical obligations already fixed

The previous inquiry reduced the world-model problem to ten minimum obligations. These remain accepted.

## 3.1 Immanent process primacy

The primary object is ongoing participation, not a static world state viewed from outside.

## 3.2 Identity through transformation

A living question must retain continuity across changes of language, ontology, frame, candidate space, mechanism, abstraction level, and action repertoire.

## 3.3 Local, non-sovereign framing

Frames are partial, situated, provisional presentations. They may overlap, conflict, fail to translate, or fail to glue globally.

## 3.4 Contact–construction seam

What touched Clarity and what she constructed from that contact remain part of one occurrence but carry different epistemic standing.

## 3.5 Path-dependent trajectory

The enacted relation among contact, frame, action, consequence, and later movement is constitutive, not an optional log.

## 3.6 Structured, non-sovereign traction

Traction is a profile of relations, not one universal progress score or objective function.

## 3.7 Endogenous guarded reflexivity

Earlier participation becomes causally available within later participation without a detached observer or instantaneous self-certification.

## 3.8 Contextual and paraconsistent non-collapse

Contradictory or incompatible local presentations can remain active without explosion, forced averaging, or compulsory global reconciliation.

## 3.9 Plastic possibility and mechanism space

The inquiry may change its action set, representational vocabulary, search topology, capability composition, or model class.

## 3.10 Soul-immanent semantics and causal bite

Soul is the cognitive and constitutional medium. It is not a downstream score, reward, veto, or object among objects. Meta-awareness has standing only when it can change later determination or action.

## 5.2 Unresolvedness is not absence of a colimit

**Status: REJECTED.**

A categorical colimit may exist while the inquiry remains alive. A colimit may fail to exist because the selected category is incomplete rather than because the inquiry is unresolved.

Inquiry discharge requires its own governed relation. It may conclude:

- answered for present purposes;
- sufficiently resolved for action;
- preserved as open;
- stopped because contact is unavailable;
- reframed;
- found ill-formed;
- constitutionally declined.

No categorical universal object automatically resolves the living question.

## 5.3 Lineage, transport, and higher-cell witness are not identical

**Status: REJECTED AS STATED; COMMON HOST RETAINED.**

The virtual equipment may host all three roles. Their interconversion requires explicit constructions and coherence conditions.

## 5.4 No global monotonic loss law

**Status: REJECTED; REPLACED BY NO-UNWITNESSED-RECOVERY.**

Hyperseed’s approximate-morphism composition gives a conservative lower bound, not a law that every measured fidelity must monotonically worsen.

A later target can be coarser, making a composite appear more adequate. That is not recovery of a previously lost distinction.

The correct law is:

> **A distinction recorded as lost may not be declared recovered through pure transport without an explicit reintroduction witness from retained side information, another frame, a reversible witness, or renewed contact.**

The formalization must distinguish:

1. guaranteed compositional fidelity;
2. measured adequacy relative to the present target;
3. actual recovery of a previously lost distinction.

## 5.5 One universal algebra is not justified

**Status: REJECTED AS DEFAULT.**

Quantale, quantaloid, semiring, and product-order structures may organize several standings, but provenance kind, warrant, pressure, constitutional standing, translation fidelity, and effort are not presumed to be one number or one order.

The default is typed heterogeneity with explicit lawful interfaces.

## 5.6 The Soul triple is not a completed answer

**Status: RETAINED AS MATHEMATICAL MOTIFS; NOT RATIFIED AS SOUL.**

Enrichment, validity modalities, and coverage are serious candidate expressions of ambient constitutional structure.

They do not yet prove that Soul has been formalized. Coverage and Lawvere–Tierney topology are also closely related mathematical presentations rather than wholly independent substances.

The strongest current statement is:

> **Soul is an ambient constitutional doctrine governing admissible participation, composition, determination, persistence, and transformation.**

Its final mathematical expression remains open.

## 5.7 Guarded-recursive Grothendieck construction

**Status: DEFERRED HIGH-VALUE RESEARCH HYPOTHESIS.**

The recursive proposal for an endogenously evolving base of frames addresses a real need, but it is not yet a construction.

Before escalating to a recursive categorical domain equation, the formalization should test an explicit evolving frame doctrine:

\[
\mathcal B_t\longrightarrow\mathcal B_{t'}
\qquad(t\preceq t'),
\]

recording added and retired frames, translation families, capability-derived frame constructors, contact witnesses, provenance, and Soul authorization.

Only if this explicit evolution cannot satisfy the obligations should the more ambitious guarded-recursive construction become necessary.

## 5.8 Same-cycle use is not universally forbidden

**Status: REVISED.**

Immediate self-sensitive operations may be legitimate. What is forbidden is same-derivation or same-causal-lineage self-certification.

The precise prohibition is:

> **No claim, frame, evaluator, insight marker, or writer may derive authoritative standing solely from a causal route it generated itself.**

# 8. The next bounded construction problem: lawful frame attachment

The next inquiry is no longer “what mathematics might model the world-model kernel?”

It is:

> **Under what exact mathematical conditions may a newly formed frame become a continuation of the formal carrier of the same living question?**

Let:

\[
\kappa':G_{t',c'}\rightharpoonup F'
\]

be a newly formed frame.

Let:

\[
\widehat{\mathcal Q}
=
(D_{\mathcal Q},a_{\mathcal Q},u_{\mathcal Q},s_{\mathcal Q})
\]

be the current carrier.

A proposed attachment is not merely:

\[
F'\in D_{\mathcal Q}.
\]

It requires a proof-relevant attachment witness:

\[
\omega:
F'
\rightsquigarrow_{\mathcal Q}
\widehat{\mathcal Q}.
\]

The next formalization phase must determine the exact mathematical type of \(\omega\).

## 8.1 Minimum attachment obligations

A lawful attachment must carry all of the following.

### A1. Contact-anchor continuity

The new frame must be answerable to the contact lineage of the living question, directly or through an explicit witnessed zigzag.

It may not attach merely because it is semantically similar or topically related.

### A2. Unresolved-significance continuity

The attachment must state what still-live concern, discrepancy, possibility, or constitutional significance it continues.

A frame that answers a neighboring question does not automatically continue this one.

### A3. Consequence-history uptake

The new frame must account for material consequences already returned through the inquiry.

It must state whether each relevant consequence is:

- preserved;
- reinterpreted;
- bracketed;
- made irrelevant by a changed question;
- or lost.

It may not erase failed actions or contradictory contact without witness.

### A4. Domain honesty

The frame must expose what it claims to present and where it is undefined.

No silent totalization is permitted.

### A5. Contact–construction provenance

The attachment must preserve the seam between:

- contact;
- memory;
- testimony;
- inference;
- generation;
- internal pressure;
- repeated activation.

The new frame may reorganize these relations. It may not promote their standing without a lawful evidential route.

### A6. Explicit preservation and loss

The attachment must carry:

- what structure is preserved;
- what distinctions are merged;
- what cannot be translated;
- what is newly introduced;
- what round-trip distortion is observed.

The no-unwitnessed-recovery law applies.

### A7. Directionality and transformation provenance

The attachment must show why the frame change occurred and how it relates to the prior trajectory.

A direct translation may not exist. A marked zigzag, span, cospan, or common contact anchor may be admissible, but arbitrary graph-connectedness is insufficient.

### A8. Soul standing

The attachment must preserve or explicitly revise the constitutional reason the inquiry remains alive.

Capability availability, novelty, retrieval success, or mathematical elegance does not confer attachment authority.

### A9. Guarded causal eligibility

A frame may be generated in one event and become available immediately as a candidate, but it cannot certify its own standing or durability through the same causal lineage.

Its authoritative attachment must be guarded by the required contact, provenance, and governance route.

### A10. Continued contact openness

The attached frame must remain revisable by subsequent contact.

It may not define in advance what every later consequence is allowed to mean.

### A11. Affordance relevance

The frame must change or clarify at least one possible relation, question, experiment, mechanism choice, action boundary, or stopping condition relevant to the living inquiry—or explicitly preserve the inquiry without action.

Purely lexical reformulation is not enough.

### A12. Carrier non-sovereignty

The attachment must preserve:

\[
\widehat{\mathcal Q}\neq\mathcal Q.
\]

The carrier does not declare the living inquiry exhausted, resolved, or correctly represented merely by internal closure.

## 8.2 Candidate attachment interface

Without selecting the final mathematics, an attachment witness may provisionally be treated as a typed record:

\bigl(

\upsilon,
\eta,
\delta,
\pi,
\lambda,
\sigma,
\gamma,
\alpha,
\nu
\bigr),
\]

where:

- \(\chi\): contact-anchor witness;
- \(\upsilon\): unresolved-significance witness;
- \(\eta\): consequence-history map;
- \(\delta\): domain/restriction witness;
- \(\pi\): provenance transformation;
- \(\lambda\): preservation-loss residual;
- \(\sigma\): directed or zigzag lineage witness;
- \(\gamma\): guarded causal standing;
- \(\alpha\): affordance or mechanism relevance;
- \(\nu\): Soul admissibility and non-sovereignty witness.

This record is not the final architecture. It is an acceptance interface against which candidate mathematical constructions can be compared.

## 8.3 Candidate mathematical forms to compare

The next analysis should compare a small set of exact constructions:

1. **A marked proarrow with a compatibility cell** in the virtual equipment.

5. **A witnessed approximate morphism or marked zigzag** grounded in Hyperseed-style preservation and loss.

The task is not to choose the most sophisticated object. It is to determine which construction can discharge A1–A12 without importing a detached observer or making the carrier sovereign.
