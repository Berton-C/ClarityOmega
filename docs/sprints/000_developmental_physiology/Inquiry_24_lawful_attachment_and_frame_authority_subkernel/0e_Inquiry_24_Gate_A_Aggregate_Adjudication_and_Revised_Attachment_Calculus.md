# 3. The contact-anchor module: exact type, variance, and write boundary

Let \(\mathcal V\) be the typed quantaloid or equivalent enriched standing base used by \(\mathbb E\).

\[
a_{\mathcal Q}(c,f)
\in
\mathcal V\bigl(|f|,|c|\bigr)

expresses the typed standing with which \(f\) answers to \(c\).

## 3.2 Variance

The module is:

- **contravariant in the frame presentation**: a reframing or restriction acts by precomposition and cannot transmit more answerability than its witnessed relation carries;
- **covariant in the contact lineage**: a contact refinement or later contact continuation acts by postcomposition and preserves the contact provenance route.

Using the selected composition convention, the module laws include:

\[
a_{\mathcal Q}(c,f)
\otimes
\mathsf F_{\mathcal Q}(f',f)
\leq
a_{\mathcal Q}(c,f'),

and:

\[
\mathsf C_{\mathcal Q}(c,c')
\otimes
a_{\mathcal Q}(c,f)
\leq
a_{\mathcal Q}(c',f).
\]

These inequalities do not say that topical similarity makes a frame part of the inquiry. They state how an already witnessed answerability relation is transported.

## 3.3 Proposed answerability of a new frame

When it occurs as the source of a proarrow below, \(F'\) denotes the one-object \(\mathcal V\)-category generated by the newly formed frame presentation.

The corresponding virtual-equipment cell is the attachment spine \(\omega_0\). Non-bottom grade is necessary but not sufficient. Membership additionally requires live-significance continuation, consequence uptake, provenance, lifecycle standing, and Soul-doctrine judgment.

## 3.4 Anchor-write law

This is the structural seam between contact and construction:

A live item \(\ell\in U_{\mathcal Q}\) carries:

- a pointer to contact lineage;
- a statement of the still-live discrepancy, possibility, obligation, or concern;
- consequence-history references;
- typed standing \(\Gamma(\ell)\);
- a lifecycle status such as live, suspended, provisionally discharged, or retired.

Define:

\[
\boxed{
\operatorname{CarrierOpen}
\bigl(\widehat{\mathcal Q}_e\bigr)
\iff
\exists \ell\in U_{\mathcal Q}^e:
\operatorname{LiveAt}(\ell,e)
\wedge
\operatorname{AnchorGrounded}(\ell,a_{\mathcal Q})
}
\]

This is a positive property of the formal carrier. It means that the carrier currently represents at least one grounded live item.

## 5.2 What CarrierOpen does not establish

Neither direction of the following equivalence is admitted:

\[
\operatorname{CarrierOpen}
\bigl(\widehat{\mathcal Q}\bigr)
\not\Longleftrightarrow
\operatorname{LivingQuestionOpen}(\mathcal Q).
\]

Specifically:

\[
\neg\operatorname{CarrierOpen}
\bigl(\widehat{\mathcal Q}\bigr)
\not\Rightarrow
\operatorname{Closed}(\mathcal Q).
\]

The carrier may have lost, failed to surface, misclassified, or prematurely discharged a live item. Absence of represented open standing is not proof that the living inquiry is exhausted.

Likewise, a current action may be sufficiently determined while the inquiry remains open.

## 5.3 Living-question discharge as a separate doctrine judgment

where \(o\in\mathsf{Outcome}\) and \(w\) supplies the required contact, consequence, provenance, and Soul route.

Discharge is not derivable from:

- existence of a colimit;

Even a lawful discharge does not assert that a formal representation has exhausted actuality. It records the Soul-doctrine standing of the inquiry for the present purpose and trajectory.

# 6. Four attachment lifecycle standings

The original candidate/authoritative split is insufficient. The revised calculus distinguishes four standings.

## 6.1 Candidate

\[
\operatorname{Candidate}
(F',\widehat{\mathcal Q},e_f)
\]

means that \(F'\) has been formed and may be inspected, compared, translated, challenged, or retained as a possibility.

Candidate standing:

- does not make \(F'\) a member of \(\mathsf F_{\mathcal Q}\);
- does not authorize an action;
- does not confer contact standing;
- does not require a complete attachment record;
- may exist in the same event in which the frame was generated.

## 6.2 Probe-eligible

\[
\operatorname{ProbeEligible}
(F',\widehat{\mathcal Q},A,e_p)
\]

means that \(F'\) may guide the bounded, reversible, information-seeking action \(A\).

Probe eligibility requires:

- a complete prospective attachment package \(\omega^+\);
- domain honesty;
- anchor and live-significance continuation;
- no provenance promotion;
- a declared prospective affordance \(\alpha^+\) or an explicit preserve-without-action marker;
- boundedness and reversibility of \(A\);
- Soul-doctrine admissibility for the probe.

Probe eligibility does not establish that the frame is true, authoritative, or insightful.

## 6.3 Authoritative

\[
\operatorname{Authoritative}
(F',\widehat{\mathcal Q},e_a)
\]

means that \(F'\) may participate as an attached frame in current Soul determination within its declared domain.

Authority requires:

- prior candidate standing;
- prior or current probe eligibility;
- strict proto-time separation from frame formation;
- materially relevant contact outside the frame’s self-generation cone;
- consequence-history uptake;
- a lawful attachment act by the governed attachment writer;
- Soul-doctrine judgment.

Authority is scoped. It does not make the frame globally true, total, or durable.

## 6.4 Durable

\[
\operatorname{Durable}
(F',\widehat{\mathcal Q},e_d)
\]

means that later trajectory supports retaining \(F'\), its transformation witness, or its learned consequence relations as a persistent developmental resource.

Durability requires later evidence of:

- exercised consequence rather than merely predicted affordance;
- continued contact permeability;
- no provenance promotion;
- no unwitnessed loss recovery;
- retained living-question lineage;
- changed or clarified future participation;
- governed writer approval under Soul.

Durable does not mean immutable or canonical truth. Later contact may suspend, demote, revise, or retire the frame.

## 6.5 Lifecycle transition discipline

The primary transition graph is:

\[
\operatorname{Candidate}
\longrightarrow
\operatorname{ProbeEligible}
\longrightarrow
\operatorname{Authoritative}
\longrightarrow
\operatorname{Durable}.
\]

No state writes or promotes itself. Every arrow is a separate governed constructor with a typed witness.

The calculus also admits governed transitions to:

- suspended;
- rejected-for-this-inquiry;
- demoted;
- retired;
- or candidate-for-a-neighboring-inquiry.

The lifecycle is therefore not an irreversible confidence ladder. It is an authority and persistence discipline that remains answerable to contact.

# 8. Revised attachment calculus W0*–W12

## W0* — No authority from internal closure

Universal properties are permitted as local mathematical tools. They may compute restrictions, tabulations, pullbacks, residuals, or candidate factorizations.

No rule is admitted of the form:

\[
\operatorname{UniversalFactorization}
(F',\widehat{\mathcal Q})
\Rightarrow
\operatorname{ProbeEligible},
\operatorname{Authoritative},
\operatorname{Durable},
\text{or }
\operatorname{Discharge}.
\]

Record completeness, internal closure, categorical terminality, and automatic factorization never confer cognitive authority.

## W1 — Witnessed anchor-spine

A prospective attachment package requires a virtual-equipment cell \(\omega_0\) whose source is a finite marked path or zigzag of admissible relations and whose target is the proposed anchor-answerability component \(\alpha_{F'}\).

The spine must exhibit:

- a route to \(a_{\mathcal Q}\) or direct renewed contact;
- a proof-relevant anchor comparison \(\chi\);
- no claim stronger than the route’s typed grade permits.

Non-bottom answerability is necessary and not sufficient.

## W2 — Stage-typed completeness

A frame may be a candidate without a complete attachment record.

Probe eligibility requires every field of \(\omega^+\) to be present and well typed. At that point:

- \(\upsilon\) is nonempty;
- \(\eta\) is total on the consequences declared material under the governing materiality judgment;
- \(\delta\) exhibits the frame’s restriction and competence;
- \(\alpha^+\) is present or an explicit preserve-without-action marker is supplied.

Authority and durability require their additional later packages \(\epsilon^-\) and \(\rho^-\). A prospective record cannot fill those fields in advance.

## W3 — Non-promoting typed standing arithmetic

For every standing transformation \(\pi\):

- provenance kind never promotes;
- a warrant increase cites a lawful material evidence route;
- pressure, repetition, salience, fluency, and self-consistency never increase external warrant;
- support cones compose by explicit union of cited dependencies;
- freshness is updated by its own temporal law;
- local scalar projections, where permitted, are scope-typed and confer no Soul authority.

## W4 — Anchored zigzag discipline

Every leg of a path or zigzag must carry:

- contact-answerability standing;
- at least one live-significance continuation, unless the route is explicitly direct renewed contact;
- preservation/loss data;
- transformation provenance.

An intermediate presentation generated in the same self-generation cone as the proposed attachment cannot provide independent authority.

Distinct inquiries are not merged by graph connectedness, vocabulary overlap, or frame similarity. Inquiry merging requires a separate:

\[
\operatorname{IdentifyAnchor}
(a_{\mathcal Q_1},a_{\mathcal Q_2},w)
\]

judgment grounded in contact and Soul standing.

## W5 — Four-standing guarded lifecycle

Candidacy is immediate after lawful frame formation.

Probe eligibility requires \(\omega^+\) and a bounded reversible probe.

Authority requires:

\[
e_f\prec e_a
\]

and materially relevant contact outside \(\operatorname{SelfGen}(\omega^+)\).

Durability requires later retrospective evidence \(\rho^-\).

No lifecycle standing may certify or write its own successor.

## W6 — Witness before governed act

The calculus distinguishes the evidence object from every state-changing act.

The only lifecycle writers are governed constructors such as:

- `admitCandidate`;
- `authorizeProbe`;
- `attachAuthoritative`;
- `promoteDurable`;
- `suspendFrame`;
- `demoteFrame`;
- `retireFrame`.

A renderer, generator, evaluator, attachment record, frame, or carrier-internal closure cannot call an authoritative or durable writer merely because it produced the relevant data.

## W7 — Historical conservativity and additive interpretation

Historical contact, action, and consequence records are immutable.

For each frame \(F\), consequence \(h\), classification \(k\), witness \(w\), and event \(e\), interpretation is stored as:

\[
\operatorname{Interp}_{\mathcal Q}
(F,h,k,w,e).
\]

A new frame appends new frame-indexed interpretations. It never rewrites or deletes prior interpretations.

The lawful extension inclusion:

\[
\iota:
\widehat{\mathcal Q}
\hookrightarrow
\widehat{\mathcal Q}'
\]

must preserve:

- prior contact events;
- prior action and consequence events;
- prior provenance;
- prior frame-indexed interpretations;
- prior anchor components.

Reinterpretation is therefore additive and paraconsistent, not retroactive mutation.

## W8 — Global non-amalgamation and local joint-use compatibility

Attachment membership imposes no pairwise consistency condition on all frames and requires no global colimit or master presentation.

However, joint use has its own scoped judgment:

\[
\Gamma
\vdash_{\mathfrak S}
\operatorname{JointUse}
(F_i,F_j,A\mid \kappa),
\]

where \(\kappa\) is either:

- a local compatibility cell sufficient for action \(A\); or
- an explicit tension witness preserving the incompatibility while governing how the frames may jointly constrain \(A\).

No local witness means no joint composition. The frames may still coexist in the carrier.

## W9 — No-unwitnessed-recovery ledger discipline

The loss ledger \(L_{\mathcal Q}\) is append-only.

Pure transport, target coarsening, high approximate-morphism degree, or favorable round-trip measurement cannot remove a loss entry.

The only constructor that restores availability is:

\[
