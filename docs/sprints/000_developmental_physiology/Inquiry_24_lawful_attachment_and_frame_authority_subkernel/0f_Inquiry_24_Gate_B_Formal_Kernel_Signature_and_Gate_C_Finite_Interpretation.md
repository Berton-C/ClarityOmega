## 0.1 Repair B1 — loss and recovery require a context index

The Gate A notation

\[
\operatorname{Lost}(d,e)
\]

is under-typed. A distinction is not normally lost everywhere. It is lost **under a translation, frame, or composed working context**. The formal signature therefore uses:

\[
\operatorname{LostIn}(d,\tau,e)
\]

and:

\[
\operatorname{RecoveredIn}(d,\xi,e),
\]

where \(\tau\) identifies the translation or attachment context that lost the distinction and \(\xi\) identifies the context into which it was later reintroduced.

This repair prevents a frame-local loss from being mistaken for global absence and makes the no-unwitnessed-recovery theorem type-correct.

## 0.2 Repair B2 — lifecycle standing is append-only history, not a mutable status field

Candidate, ProbeEligible, Authoritative, Durable, Suspended, Demoted, and Retired are represented by append-only lifecycle events in the historical substructure. The current standing is a derived relation over that event history.

This avoids a mutable lifecycle object whose previous standing could disappear. It also lets one prove lifecycle non-collapse from the constructor inventory.

A frame may be formed at one event and acquire a complete prospective attachment package only after later trajectory, interpretation, or capability results become available. The formal witness is therefore indexed by:

\[
