## 2. Question 9.1: the exact type of the attachment witness

### 2.1 The anchor module (prerequisite)

**Kind: proposal, using only admitted structure.**

Let D_Q be the carrier's guarded open diagram, with presentations F_0, F_1, ... among its vertices. Define the **contact-anchor module**:

a_Q assigns to each presentation F_i a typed, graded value a_Q(F_i): the standing with which F_i answers to the inquiry's originating and accumulated contact lineage. The values live in the typed standing family Gamma of 0c 4.3 (provenance kind, warrant, dependence, pressure, freshness), not in one scalar; the fidelity components are quantaloid-valued and compose with proarrows by the equipment's action (restriction along a proarrow composes grades by the quantaloid tensor).

Two properties are definitional:

- **A module is acted on, not pointed at.** Every proarrow into or out of a presentation induces a restriction of a_Q along it. But a_Q has no vertex in the diagram: no frame contains it, no frame can present it, and therefore no frame can capture or redefine it. Answerability is something presentations **have toward** the lineage, not a thing inside any frame's ontology.
- **Nonzero anchor grade is what membership means.** A presentation belongs to this inquiry exactly insofar as a_Q assigns it non-bottom standing. The anchor module individuates the inquiry (this is the load-bearing fact for 9.4).

### 2.2 The spine: a cell with composite source

**Kind: literature (virtual equipment cells) applied as proposal.**

A virtual equipment's cells natively have a finite list of proarrows as horizontal source, without requiring that list to compose to a single proarrow. This is exactly the zigzag shape A7 anticipates, and it also natively handles multi-strand attachment (a new frame continuing several prior presentations at once).

The spine of the witness is a cell:

omega_0: (p_1, ..., p_n) double-arrow a-restriction,

where p_1, ..., p_n is the chain of graded, partial frame relations (Hyperseed approximate morphisms and marked zigzag legs, per 0c 6.3 and 8.3 candidate 5) linking F' through intermediate presentations to presentations already in D_Q, and the target is the restriction of the anchor module along that chain. Read plainly: **the cell witnesses that F', through this specific chain, inherits non-bottom answerability to this inquiry's contact lineage.** No chain, no cell. No cell, no attachment, however similar the frames look.

## 3. Question 9.2: the contact anchor without objectification

The answer is Distinction 2, now stated in full.

**The anchor is typed as a module over the carrier diagram, never as a vertex in it.** Three consequences do the work the question asks for:

1. **No frame can capture the anchor.** A frame's ontology contains what the frame presents. The anchor is not presented by any frame; it is the graded answerability each presentation bears toward the lineage. Frames relate **to** it; nothing relates **as** it. The failure mode 006 Section 10 describes (representation deciding what contact is allowed to mean) would require the representation to stand where the anchor stands, and the typing makes that ill-formed rather than forbidden.
2. **The anchor is not a fixed ontology.** Its content grows as contact accrues: each new contact event extends the lineage the module answers to, by ordinary carrier growth, guarded like everything else. What is fixed is the module's **role**, not its content. This is how the frame ecology can change radically (0c 3.9) while the inquiry remains the same inquiry: frames churn; the answerability structure persists and extends.
3. **Extension is conservative on the anchor.** When an attachment act adjoins F', the extended anchor module restricts to the old one on old presentations (this is one clause of the Section 9 conservativity theorem). No attachment retroactively changes what prior presentations answered to, except through eta's explicit reinterpretation entries, which are witnessed by construction.

One honest boundary: the judgment "this frame bears non-bottom answerability to that contact lineage" has an irreducible semantic component in general (does this reformulation still touch that original discrepancy). Section 8.3 names this as one of the semantic-evaluation seams, with the substrate-defines, renderer-evaluates, substrate-enforces split applied to it explicitly.

## 4. Question 9.3: open standing as a positive datum

**Kind: proposal, elementary structure.**

0c 5.2 rejects colimit absence as the meaning of unresolvedness and lists the discharge outcomes. The structure that carries this without categorical exotica:

**u_Q is a set of live items, each a positive datum**: a still-open discrepancy, unmet possibility, standing obligation, or constitutional concern, each carrying (i) a pointer into the anchor module (what contact it answers to), (ii) a pointer into the consequence history (what has already been returned about it), and (iii) its own typed standing Gamma (in particular its pressure and freshness components: this is where DemandPersistence in the stuck signature of 0c 4.5 lives, giving that conjunct a formal home rather than a mood).

**The inquiry is open if and only if u_Q contains at least one live item with non-bottom anchor grade.** Openness is the presence of something, never the absence of a universal object.

**Discharge is a separate judgment form**, one per outcome in 0c 5.2's list (answered for present purposes, sufficiently resolved for action, preserved as open, stopped for lack of contact, reframed, found ill-formed, constitutionally declined). Each discharge form has its own premises: at minimum a contact or consequence route and Soul standing; none is derivable from carrier-internal closure, because no constructor targets discharge from closure (Section 2.3, side condition nu). The standing of the whole inquiry is then a derived labeled state over the live-item set: a small labeled transition system whose transitions are exactly the discharge judgments.

This also completes upsilon's type in Section 2.3: attachment continuity of significance (A2) is a map on live items, which is checkable, rather than a claim about topical resemblance, which is not.

## 5. Question 9.4: zigzags without collapse

A discontinuous reframing may attach through a chain that passes through intermediate presentations, including span-shaped legs into a common third presentation, with no direct translation from any prior frame to F'. The question is what disciplines the chain so that connectedness does not merge distinct inquiries.

**Three admissibility conditions on the chain, jointly (proposal):**

1. **Anchor preservation per leg.** Every intermediate presentation in the chain must itself bear non-bottom answerability to this inquiry's anchor module, witnessed, at the leg's formation. A chain may not tunnel through presentations that answer to nothing or to a different lineage. This kills the bridge-concept attack: a generated intermediate with no anchor grade contributes bottom to the composite by the quantaloid arithmetic, and the spine cell fails to exist.
2. **Significance transport per leg.** upsilon must factor through the chain: each leg carries forward at least one live item's continuation. A chain that transmits answerability but drops every live item connects surfaces, not inquiries.
3. **Guarded formation of intermediates.** Intermediate presentations invented in the same derivation as the attachment claim are candidates like everything else; they contribute to an authoritative attachment only under the same two-part guard (Section 7). One cannot manufacture the middle of one's own zigzag and certify through it in one causal breath.

**Why distinct inquiries cannot collapse (the individuation principle):** inquiries are individuated by their anchor modules, and anchor modules are not frame content. Two carriers merge only through an **anchor identification**: a witnessed, contact-grounded judgment that their lineages answer to one unresolved contact history (for example, the discovery that two separately pursued symptoms share one cause, witnessed by the contact that disclosed the sharing). Frame-level similarity, shared vocabulary, or graph adjacency have no constructor targeting anchor identification. Arbitrary connectedness can therefore relate frames endlessly without ever merging lineages, because merging is not a connectedness property at all: it is a separate, guarded, contact-requiring judgment, rare by construction, exactly as it should be.

## 6. Question 9.5: degrees, grades, residuals, and the recovery law

The four quantities are kept distinct, each with its verified mathematical home, and their lawful interfaces stated:

1. **Approximate-morphism degree (per leg).** Hyperseed Definition 216, verified this session: the meet over node pairs of the residuum from source weight to image weight; f is a q-approximate morphism when q lies below the degree. This is a **guarantee**: it bounds what the leg preserves, worst case, by the residuation adjunction.
2. **Distributor grade (per leg, generalized).** The equipment-level generalization: the leg's value in the **typed standing family**, of which the Definition 216 degree is the fidelity component. Per 0c 5.5 there is no single order; the fidelity component composes by tensor, the provenance component composes by the non-promotion arithmetic, the dependence component composes by union of cones (Section 7). 3. **Guaranteed composite fidelity.** Theorem 14, verified this session: the composite degree is bounded below by the tensor of leg degrees, and, as the source itself stresses, the composite **may be better** than the tensor, for instance when a later target is coarser. This is precisely why 0c 5.4 rejected my earlier global monotone loss law, and the rejection is accepted here in full: the tensor bound is a floor, not a trajectory.
4. **Measured adequacy versus actual recovery.** Where a reverse transport exists, the round-trip residual is a **measurement** against the identity: it can refine the guaranteed floor upward. What it can never do is convert into **recovery** of a distinction the loss ledger records as lost. A coarser target making a composite look faithful is adequacy relative to that target; the lost distinction is still lost.

**The no-unwitnessed-recovery law as a typing discipline (answering the question's second half): yes, and with a derived theorem.**

- **Discipline:** the loss ledger is append-only except through one constructor, **reintroduce**, whose type requires, as an argument, a witness from one of exactly the sources 0c 5.4 names: retained side information attached to the ledger entry, another attached frame that still carries the distinction, a reversible witness, or a renewed contact event. Pure transport has no constructor targeting the ledger. Measured adequacy has no constructor targeting the ledger. The illegal move is not forbidden; it is unwritable, the same design pattern as provenance non-promotion.
- **Derived theorem (statement and proof shape):** in any carrier built by the lawful constructors, every distinction that is both marked lost at some event and available at a later event has a reintroduction witness in its causal history. Proof by structural induction over carrier construction: the only constructor that makes a ledger entry unavailable-to-available is reintroduce, and reintroduce carries the witness as an argument; every other constructor preserves ledger state. The theorem is exactly as strong as the constructor inventory is closed, which is a Gate B obligation: the minimal kernel signature must enumerate the constructors and nothing else may write.

## 7. Question 9.6: the two-part guard

0c 4.2's repair is accepted as the specification: the later modality supplies not-now; it does not supply not-self-produced. The guard therefore has two parts, and the verified Hyperseed proto-time material shows they are two projections of one causal structure rather than two bolted-together mechanisms.

**Setting.** Proto-time (T, <) is a strict partial order on inquiry events (Definition 56, verified). For an event e, write cone(e) for the downward closure of e. Separately, each item carries a **dependence cone** Dep(x): the set of events its derivation actually used, downward-closed along derivation edges only. Dep is the dependence component of the typed standing Gamma, now given exact content: a subset of T, composed under inference by union.

**The guard on authoritative attachment (proposal):** omega formed at event e becomes authoritative at event e' only if:

1. **Temporal part (not-yet):** e < e' in the causal order; e' is strictly later, and where the order is only partial, incomparable events do not qualify: the guard never infers order that proto-time does not warrant (0c 6.1).
2. **Independence part (not-only-self):** the evidence discharging the obligation at e' has a dependence cone not contained in the derivation-only cone of e. At least one event in Dep(evidence) lies outside what the claim's own derivation generated. Delayed repetition of a self-produced claim fails this part while passing the first, which is exactly the failure 0c 4.2 names.

**Formally:** authoritative(omega, e') requires e < e' and Dep(evidence(e')) not-a-subset-of dcone(e), where dcone(e) is the derivation-generated downward closure of e.

**Two refinements the verified sources make available:**

- **Evidential precedence.** Hyperseed Definition 57 makes the before-relation itself p-bit-valued: precedence can carry positive and negative evidence simultaneously. Guard eligibility is therefore itself a standing-bearing judgment, not a boolean read off a clock: in asynchronous or branching stretches of the inquiry, "strictly later along the relevant causal order" may be held with graded, even conflicting, evidence, and the doctrine can require the positive component to clear the doctrine's own admissibility rather than pretending the order is total. The linear cycle index remains what 0c 6.1 says it is: a computational shadow (Theorem 5's linear extensions exist for finite proto-times), never the formal ground.
- **Candidacy is untouched.** Per 0c 5.8, nothing above restricts same-event use of a freshly formed frame as a candidate: consideration, comparison, even provisional action under the frame's own declared domain are immediate. Only **authoritative standing and durability** wait on the guard. The prohibition is same-lineage self-certification, and the two-part guard is its exact formalization: the second part is the lineage condition, the first part gives contact time to arrive.

**A10 and UpdatePermeability under the guard.** Continued contact openness cannot be established at attachment time; it is a future-facing obligation. Its type is the guard's shape reused: the attached frame carries a standing obligation whose discharge events are later consequence-uptake demonstrations, and whose chronic non-discharge is precisely what feeds the RelevantDistinctionDeath conjunct of the stuck signature (0c 4.5). Attachment does not certify permeability; trajectory demonstrates or refutes it.

## 9. Question 9.8: coherence, existence, and growth without gluing

**The carrier is an inductively generated structure.** Its constructor inventory: found-inquiry (creates a carrier from an originating contact and first live item), record-contact, record-action-consequence, form-frame (kappa', partial, restriction-bearing), attach (the act licensed by a guard-discharged omega), reintroduce (Section 6), and the discharge family (Section 4). Nothing else writes.

**Coherence conditions (the answer's first half):**

1. **Event ordering.** Every constructor application is an event in proto-time; an omega's spine may reference only presentations already in the carrier at events strictly below its formation event. No retro-attachment.
2. **Ledger monotonicity.** Loss ledgers and consequence records are cumulative; the only unavailable-to-available transition is reintroduce.
3. **Anchor conservativity.** The extended anchor module restricts to the prior anchor module on prior presentations.

**The minimal theorem (the answer's second half), in two clauses:**

> **Conservativity of extension.** For every lawful attachment act, the inclusion of the old carrier into the extended carrier preserves and reflects all standing judgments about old material; no attachment changes the standing of prior contact, consequence, or live items except through its own explicit, witnessed eta entries.

> **Non-amalgamation.** The attachment rule's premises mention F', the anchor, the live items, the consequence history, and the standings. They mention **no compatibility condition between F' and any other attached frame.** Therefore carriers containing pairwise incompatible frames exist for every lawful attachment sequence, and growth never forces a global frame, a colimit, or reconciliation.

**Proof shapes.** Conservativity: structural induction over the constructor inventory; only attach touches old material, and only through eta, which is witnessed by type; all other constructors are additive. Non-amalgamation: immediate by inspection of the rule, then exhibited concretely by the finite model below, where two frames that contradict each other about the cause are both lawfully attached. The slogan form, which the aggregate analysis can carry forward as a design invariant:

> **Compatibility is required vertically, frame to anchor, never horizontally, frame to frame.**

This is Obligation 3.8 discharged inside the attachment law itself, rather than by a separate paraconsistency mechanism: contradiction persists in the carrier because nothing ever asked the frames to agree; they were only ever asked to answer to the same contact.
