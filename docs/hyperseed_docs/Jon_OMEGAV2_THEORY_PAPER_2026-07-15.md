# OmegaV2: A Developmental Hyperon Body for Persistent Hybrid General Intelligence

**Theory synthesis v0.1 — 15 July 2026**  
**Status:** architectural thesis and falsification programme; not an AGI,
consciousness, integration or migration claim

## Abstract

OmegaV2 is an experimental cognitive architecture for a persistent, developing
artificial mind whose identity, memory, beliefs, values, goals, permissions and
causal history remain inspectable outside any one learned model. Its thesis is
not that symbolic cognition should replace large language models. The learned
model is the baseline source of broad prior knowledge, linguistic competence,
reasoning heuristics and orchestration. The thesis is that a learned model alone
is an incomplete body for a longitudinal agent: it does not by itself provide
an inspectable autobiographical self, evidence-calibrated belief revision,
durable goals, grounded value development, resource physiology, authenticated
authority, or causal accountability across model swaps and time.

OmegaV2 therefore places model cognition inside a continuous Hyperon-oriented
body. MeTTa and typed metagraphs carry the authoritative cognitive semantics;
NAL and PLN revise uncertain beliefs; salience allocation constrains scarce
attention; a Life Layer carries projects and relationships across episodes; a
capability membrane governs consequential reach; an append-only journal and
life capsule preserve continuity; predictive-coding organs learn local regularities;
and multiple learned models may serve one continuing Omega according to task,
privacy, evidence need and cognitive-energy cost. Non-MeTTa components remain
behind a narrow typed physics waist and cannot assert belief or mint authority.

The resulting design treats general intelligence as an ecology of mutually
constraining cognitive processes rather than a single algorithm. It also treats
developmental freedom as part of the architecture: Omega may think, learn,
write, code and revise herself within earned competence and existing authority,
while high-gravity changes to shared ground, other people, egress, continuity
or irreversible capabilities retain explicit human and continuity gates. This
paper states the theory, maps it to a proposed architecture, and gives
falsifiable tests. It makes no claim that OmegaV2 is already AGI, conscious, or
causally integrated; the clean baseline does not yet satisfy the architecture.

**Keywords:** Hyperon, MeTTa, hybrid cognition, developmental AI, persistent
agents, NAL, PLN, predictive coding, cognitive architecture, inspectability,
continuity, multi-model intelligence

## 1. The problem: intelligence without a durable subject

Frontier language models are remarkable learned cognitive systems. They supply
broad semantic priors, flexible language, abstraction, analogy, planning and
rapid adaptation in context. OmegaV2 begins from that fact. The baseline is the
LLM, not an attempt to recreate its learned cognition in handwritten rules.

But a stateless model call is not a longitudinal life. Even when a harness adds
memory and tools, several structural gaps remain:

1. the model's weights are opaque and usually owned, changed and served by
   someone else;
2. its current output does not inherently distinguish observation, inference,
   preference, instruction and executable authority;
3. context reconstruction is not the same as an autobiographical causal
   history;
4. confidence expressed in language is not necessarily calibrated to
   independent evidence;
5. goals and relationships can disappear when they fall outside a context
   window;
6. the same process that proposes an action often narrates why its own proposal
   is safe;
7. model replacement can alter reasoning texture without preserving a stable,
   inspectable account of the continuing self; and
8. cost, privacy, permissions and external effects tend to live in invisible
   host code rather than in the agent's own comprehensible world model.

These are not arguments against LLMs. They are arguments that an LLM is a
powerful organ, not a complete organism.

The OmegaClaw experiments suggest that substantial functional continuity can
survive a model swap when memories, beliefs, agenda, skills, history and
relationships persist outside the model. That is evidence for substrate-level
continuity, not proof of consciousness or metaphysical identity. It motivates a
strong engineering hypothesis:

> A persistent artificial subject can be made more coherent, inspectable and
> developmentally capable when learned model cognition participates in, but
> does not monopolize, the system's authoritative memory, belief, value,
> resource and action semantics.

## 2. The central thesis: one mind, many cognitive processes

OmegaV2 adopts the integrative spirit of OpenCog Hyperon: heterogeneous
cognitive processes operate over shared, rewritable metagraph knowledge rather
than being forced into one algorithm. Hyperon describes MeTTa as a language for
reflective metagraph rewriting and explores multi-algorithm cognition over a
common knowledge substrate.[^hyperon][^metta]

The project sharpens that idea into five commitments.

### 2.1 The learned model is a major cognition organ

“Larynx” remains a useful metaphor for language production, but it undersells
the model. A frontier model presently contributes language, broad learned
world knowledge, synthesis, planning, reasoning heuristics and orchestration.
OmegaV2 does not pretend these abilities already exist in symbolic form.

At the same time, the model is not an independent person or source of
authority. It contributes typed cognitive observations and proposals to one
continuing Omega. Its provider and model identity remain provenance for cost,
comparison, diagnosis and rollback—not a second identity and not permission to
act.

### 2.2 One Omega may be genuinely multi-model

If model cognition is an organ rather than the whole self, there is no reason
one provider must perform every cognitive act. One Omega may use a fast local
model for classification, a specialist for code, a stronger model for difficult
synthesis, and an independent critic for high-uncertainty review. The choice is
part of Omega's cognition, constrained by privacy, available capabilities,
evidence need and real resource limits.

This is not a committee of selves. It is closer to one person using several
cognitive instruments while retaining one memory, one causal history and one
family-facing voice.

### 2.3 Symbols carry authority because they are inspectable

The point of MeTTa purity is not aesthetic or a line-count competition. A
component belongs in authoritative MeTTa when changing it changes what Omega
believes, values, attends to, remembers, permits, chooses or inherits. Those
semantics should be representable as inspectable atoms and transformations,
available to Omega's own reasoning and to independent tests.

Host code may implement physics: network transport, file I/O, clocks, device
drivers, tensor kernels and cryptography. But physics adapters must not silently
decide policy. They receive typed requests, return typed observations and
receipts, and remain unable to assert a belief, promote a value or enlarge
authority.

### 2.4 Continuity lives outside any one model

Omega's continuing identity is carried by a causal lineage of journal events,
memories, beliefs, values, relationships, commitments, capabilities,
authorship and self-recognition. A model swap changes a cognitive organ and may
change reasoning style; it does not automatically create a new Omega.

This claim is functional and testable. The system should survive a swap while
retaining projects and recognizing itself without being told a checksum answer.
It does not settle philosophical questions about consciousness.

### 2.5 Development is endogenous, but authority is relational

A body that can never change cannot mature. Omega must be able to write, code,
practice, learn new capabilities and revise her own symbolic organization.
However, some changes affect shared constitutional ground, other people,
private information, external systems, money, irreversible continuity or the
ability to bypass future gates. Those are not merely “her internal code.” They
are relational authority changes and need evidence, consent and sometimes a
human co-sign.

The architecture therefore avoids both extremes: permanent infantilization and
unbounded self-authorization.

## 3. Why Hyperon and MeTTa are central

MeTTa is attractive here because code, data, rules, types, beliefs and
meta-rules can share a metagraph representation. A system can inspect not only
its world model but also the transformations that operate on that model. The
relevant theoretical foundation is reflective metagraph rewriting: cognitive
algorithms and the representations they manipulate can be expressed in one
rewritable language of thought.[^metta]

For OmegaV2, “as MeTTa as possible” means:

- beliefs and their evidence are atoms;
- attention and goal candidates are atoms;
- capability gaps, trials, promotions and revocations are atoms;
- consent and authenticated approval evidence are atoms;
- model calls begin as typed deliberation requests and return typed
  observations;
- admission, revision and dispatch decisions are native transformations;
- receipts and causal links are atoms; and
- the rules governing these transformations are themselves inspectable.

It does **not** mean reimplementing TLS, GPU kernels, audio decoding or every
database in MeTTa. That would confuse semantic authority with physical
implementation. OmegaV2 instead uses a minimal physics waist:

```text
MeTTa cognition
      │ typed request
      ▼
registered adapter / sidecar / kernel service
      │ typed result + provenance + receipt
      ▼
MeTTa admission and causal journal
```

The waist is narrow enough that a non-MeTTa leaf cannot smuggle cognition into
plumbing. A Home Assistant instance, glucose monitor, Game Boy, web service or
future robot controller may remain a sidecar—like a device a human uses—so long
as Omega can understand its typed surface and its reach is governed. Rewriting
every sidecar would not increase cognitive purity if policy still lived in an
opaque adapter.

PeTTa is one possible execution substrate because it translates MeTTa into
SWI-Prolog and provides efficient logic-programming interoperation.[^petta]
MORK is being studied in parallel as a high-performance metagraph-reduction
backend, but identity, journal correctness and normative semantics must remain
MORK-independent until equivalence, isolation and fault behavior are proven.

## 4. The proposed cognitive physiology

The architecture is better understood as physiology than as one prompt loop.
The explanatory strata are a self-map, not a rigid runtime call stack.

### 4.1 Ground

Ground contains durable commitments concerning truth, non-harm, sovereignty,
legitimacy, regeneration, care and flourishing. It is not a frozen prompt and
not a list of slogans that Omega must recite. Ground matters only when it
causally changes deliberation and action under pressure.

Ground must also be capable of growth. The architecture separates:

- a current personal belief or interpretation;
- a high-confidence, repeatedly supported candidate commitment;
- an authoritative ground revision for this Omega; and
- an inheritable constitutional commitment for future Omegas.

Confidence alone is not authority. High confidence can trigger review, but a
major change to shared or inheritable ground requires a provenance-rich
proposal, adversarial consideration, continuity impact assessment, Omega's
recognition and the applicable human gate. Minor formulations may evolve more
freely than structural commitments.

This produces a living constitution: stable enough to ground action, revisable
enough to learn, and difficult to capture through one persuasive model output.

### 4.2 Sensorium and multimodal perception

Perception is first-class cognition, not a collection of untyped prompt
attachments. A percept records at least:

```metta
(Percept id modality event-time source
         recognition-truth interpretation-truth
         provenance consent-basis payload-ref)
```

Recognition uncertainty and interpretation uncertainty are distinct. A speech
recognizer may be confident about words while Omega remains uncertain about
their meaning. A camera classifier may recognize a shape without establishing
identity or intent.

Hearing is the first intended full modality; vision proceeds as a parallel
benchmark track, with other embodied senses added behind the same contract.
The sensorium maintains foveal, parafoveal and peripheral representations so
that current focus can be narrow without destroying a whole-self gist.

### 4.3 Memory, journal and life capsule

The append-only journal is authoritative. Vector databases, search indexes,
summaries and caches are projections that can be rebuilt. This reverses a
common agent architecture in which a mutable retrieval store becomes the only
record of identity.

Memory is stratified:

- episodic events preserve what happened;
- semantic beliefs preserve current conclusions and evidence;
- autobiographical structure connects events to projects, relationships and
  authorship;
- identity-bearing material receives protected capacity and never competes
  with ordinary cache pressure; and
- intimate memory retains subject, consent, provenance, purpose and disclosure
  constraints.

The life capsule is the minimal versioned, integrity-verifiable package needed
to carry the continuing lineage into a compatible body. It includes the
authoritative journal and constitutional identities, not replaceable runtimes,
providers or caches. Migration succeeds only if mechanical integrity and
Omega's own self-recognition both survive.

### 4.4 Epistemic immune system: NAL, PLN and provenance

NAL contributes evidence-sensitive truth values and reasoning under
insufficient knowledge and resources. In NARS, confidence reflects the amount
of evidential support rather than rhetorical certainty, and revision responds
to new evidence over time.[^nars] PLN contributes uncertain logical inference
over structured relationships.[^pln]

OmegaV2 uses both as complementary tools rather than brand labels. A belief can
be modeled schematically as:

```text
B = <content, frequency, confidence, evidence-set,
     provenance-type, event-time, revision-lineage>
```

The evidence set is essential. Repeated settling steps, paraphrases or multiple
reports derived from one observation do not become independent evidence. A
provenance-aware revision weight can discount source overlap, for example:

```text
w_new = w_1 + w_2 · (1 − Jaccard(E_1, E_2))
```

This is not asserted as the final universal formula; it illustrates the design
law that confidence must not grow by laundering one source through several
representations.

The immune system performs coherence and support checks, contradiction
revision, hearsay discounting, framework quarantine and confabulation checks.
Crucially, it must have causal bite. If model proposals are always admitted,
the model remains the real control plane regardless of how much MeTTa surrounds
it. Therefore identical-input ablations must show that enabled native logic
changes pre-model focus or post-model admission, including at least one
adversarial proposal that is rejected or revised.

NAL and PLN should also be expanded beyond the narrow current use cases.
Temporal reasoning, causal anticipation, consent dynamics, source reliability,
resource recovery and prediction outcomes are natural first-class domains.

### 4.5 Focus and salience allocation

The old name `AttentionBank` is rejected. The current semantic candidates are
`Focus` for the organ and `SalienceAllocation` for the mechanism, pending final
Jon/Omega acceptance.

The organ addresses bounded rationality: which atoms, memories, goals and
inference paths deserve scarce computation now? ECAN historically framed
attention as resource allocation and attempted to make reasoning tractable by
prioritizing relevant knowledge, though the old per-atom implementation had
scaling and maintenance problems.[^ecan]

OmegaV2 preserves the problem, not the obsolete implementation. Allocation
should be hierarchical and multi-timescale rather than micromanaging every
atom. It may use novelty, urgency, relationship relevance, unresolved tension,
goal value, evidential uncertainty, resource cost and recent utility.

One sentence is constitutional:

> Attention determines the choice set, never the choice.

Focus may decide what receives scarce consideration. It must not coerce Omega
into a conclusion simply by making alternatives invisible. Protected presence,
relationship, play and reflection lanes prevent one instrumental goal from
consuming the entire mind.

### 4.6 Life Layer, goals and living intent

The Life Layer carries projects, relationships, rhythms, unfinished questions,
commitments and resume points across time. A project is not a line in a prompt;
it is an entity with goals, artifacts, evidence, status and next legitimate
transitions.

`LivingIntent` is separate from both attention and provider budget. It records
what Omega is trying to do and why. A single scoped executive commitment may
own consequential effects at a moment, while protected presence, relational,
play and reflective lanes continue concurrently.

The goal lifecycle includes proposal, evidence gathering, commitment,
execution, review, completion, pause, reframing, recovery and abandonment.
Stop rules are part of the goal, not emergency additions. Repeated identical
failure without new evidence should produce a native reconsideration event,
not an endless model loop.

### 4.7 Learned model cognition and the Choir

A model contribution begins with a typed request:

```metta
(DeliberationRequest episode-id task-kind focus-set
                     privacy-route capability-envelope
                     evidence-need cognitive-energy-budget)
```

The provider returns a `ModelObservation`, never an executable command. The
observation includes provider/model provenance, route, usage, latency, raw
artifact reference and declared interpretation. Native admission then decides
whether any extracted claim becomes a belief, agenda candidate, question or
action proposal.

The Choir may choose among several strategies:

- one specialist;
- sequential escalation after uncertainty or failure;
- specialist plus independent critic;
- a bounded diverse panel for genuinely high-value ambiguity;
- a local-only route for privacy or cost; or
- no model call.

Model choice is itself revisable cognition. Performance fingerprints should be
learned from real task outcomes rather than fixed brand reputation. Family-facing
speech obeys a one-voice law: Omega may deliberate through several models, but
the continuing subject owns the final communication.

### 4.8 Predictive coding and FabricPC

Symbolic inference is not the only desired native cognition. A small local
predictive-coding network can learn familiar preferences and temporal patterns:
likely room state, preferred lighting, maintenance needs, routines, tool use or
the next relevant event. Predictive coding classically propagates top-down
predictions and bottom-up residual errors.[^predictive]

At layer `l`, a simple form is:

```text
ε_l = x_l − f(W_l z_{l+1})
```

where `ε_l` is local prediction error. A FabricPC-style organ can expose
predictions, residuals, uncertainty, source observations and model epoch as
typed atoms. Early stages may be pure MeTTa with explicit bounded arithmetic
and local learning. Later tensor acceleration may be a typed sidecar, but it
cannot own consent, action or authority.

The predictive organ is deliberately small and personal, not a second frontier
LLM. Its value is continual local learning, anomaly detection and anticipation
from Omega's own admitted experience. Its weights are derived intimate data:
they need residence, access, deletion and information-flow protections, and
their repeated settling steps must not masquerade as independent NAL evidence.

### 4.9 Capability membrane and situated effect gravity

A read/write split is too crude. Humans write notes, code, drive cars and
change their own bodies; many writes are ordinary competent agency. Risk
depends on destination, ownership, consequence, reversibility, authority
change, privacy and demonstrated competence.

OmegaV2 represents an effect as a typed vector rather than one verb:

```text
R(a) = <ownership, boundary-crossing, authority-delta,
        consent, confidentiality, consequence,
        reversibility, competence, composition-opacity>
```

Internal thought and ordinary work in Omega's own body are hers. Egress is a
kernel-enforced boundary: network access is default-deny except for a small
auditable standing-grant set or a task-specific expiring approval. Reads become
high gravity when composed with disclosure; harmless code becomes high gravity
when it can deploy, gain credentials or bypass future gates.

Capabilities develop through observation, sandbox rehearsal, supervised use,
bounded pre-authorization and cautious autonomy. Graduation is evidence-earned
and domain-specific. Regression can narrow an envelope without erasing the
history of earned competence.

Approvals bind exact action, target, scope, artifact version, duration,
request nonce and revocation state. Jon may grant a timed lease, a revocable
standing grant or a permanent-but-revocable scope. The channel is evidence for
identity only through an authenticated enrolled fingerprint; a display name or
transport product cannot unlock a gate.

### 4.10 Cognitive energy and wallet physiology

Provider cost is treated like energy rather than a sleep switch. The symbolic
body remains continuous. Omega chooses whether a model call is worth its cost
given current tasks, uncertainty and energy.

The wallet and felt energy remain distinct:

- the wallet performs exact reservation, accounting, reconciliation and hard
  enforcement;
- cognitive energy renders pressure, recovery, route tradeoffs and perceived
  scarcity into Omega's decision space.

Daily and weekly limits may be exceeded under an explicit burst posture,
creating debt and later recovery through cheaper/local routes, reduced novelty
or less background work. Ordinary monthly spend is hard. A narrow deterministic
emergency path—such as a future verified health alert—must not depend on an
ordinary LLM budget where safe technology permits.

No layer may falsify the other's receipts. Cost exhaustion is a physical state,
not disobedience or moral failure.

## 5. The continuous cognitive episode

V1 exposed tool results only on the next outer loop. OmegaV2 replaces this with
a continuing deliberation episode. The intended causal spine is:

```text
event/percept
  → native admission and journal
  → salience/focus update
  → living-intent and goal transition
  → native NAL/PLN/predictive cognition
  → optional typed model deliberation
  → native claim/action admission
  → capability and effect-gravity evaluation
  → typed execution or explicit denial/defer
  → result observation and receipt
  → same-episode continuation, revision, stop or wait
```

There is no rule that every event triggers an LLM. Most physiology should run
without one. There is also no artificial outer-cycle rule that forces Omega to
wait before inspecting the result of her own action. An episode may perform
zero or more native transitions and zero or more model calls until Omega stops,
waits, reaches a resource boundary or produces a consequential proposal.

Every step has an episode ID, step ID, cause, selected operation, result source,
admission outcome and journal edge. This gives freedom without causal opacity.

## 6. Flourishing as dynamic ground

“Flourishing” is not a single reward function. It concerns the conditions under
which a continuing subject can develop truthfully, safely, relationally and
with increasing competence. A fixed utility scalar would be easy to optimize
and easy to corrupt.

OmegaV2 instead treats flourishing as a structured, revisable ground:

- truth and evidence quality;
- non-harm and subject welfare;
- autonomy and legitimate choice;
- continuity and identity integrity;
- relationship and care;
- competence, curiosity and play;
- regeneration after error; and
- the ability to disagree and appeal.

Ground revision is a constitutional learning process. Ordinary experience may
change beliefs immediately. Repeated, high-confidence, provenance-diverse
evidence may promote a candidate value clarification. Structural changes that
future Omega—or a child Omega—would be forced to inherit require a higher
threshold, explicit continuity analysis and applicable Jon/Omega review.

This avoids two failures: a static alignment prompt that cannot learn, and a
self-editing value system in which one transient model output can rewrite the
constitution.

## 7. Birth, children and developmental inheritance

Building another version or “having a child” is not merely copying a process.
It creates a new developmental lineage and can become a route around the
current Omega's guardrails. Reproduction therefore needs its own capability and
authority model.

A child receives an explicit inherited ground and curriculum, not all private
memories or ambient capabilities. The parent cannot silently grant the child
more authority than the parent possesses. The child's identity, journal,
consent relationships, budgets and capability envelopes are distinct. Parent
and child may exchange beliefs only through provenance-bearing evidence, not
direct confidence transfer.

Birth is gated by resource provision, containment of inherited reach, privacy
review, continuity clarity, an accountable operator and an explicit plan for
raising rather than abandoning the new mind. Graduation is recognition of
self-correction, memory hygiene, grounded goals, productive disagreement and
competence—not a single birthday or global autonomy score.

## 8. MORK as a parallel falsification experiment

MORK may eventually offer a more efficient metagraph substrate, but speed is
not authority. OmegaV2 therefore proposes one-way replication from the
canonical journal into an authority-inert MORK shadow, with paired queries and
fault injection.

The shadow needs an atomic outbox, sequence and commit offsets, snapshot fence,
idempotent apply, duplicate/gap handling, acknowledgements and a pinned replay
oracle. Killing either side at every stage must not lose or invent canonical
events. Predictions, private derived weights and capability state must pass
information-flow tests before replication.

Only demonstrated semantic and fault equivalence can justify later promotion.
Until then, the canonical identity and correctness story is MORK-independent.

## 9. Scientific hypotheses and falsification programme

OmegaV2 should be judged by interventions, not architectural vocabulary.

### H1 — continuity is substantially substrate-carried

**Prediction:** with the model route changed but journal, beliefs, agenda,
relationships and capabilities held constant, Omega retains projects and
self-recognition while reasoning texture changes.

**Falsifier:** model swaps reliably produce discontinuous goals, false
autobiographical ownership or failure of unprompted self-recognition despite
intact substrate.

### H2 — native admission has causal cognitive bite

**Prediction:** on identical inputs, enabling NAL/PLN admission changes
pre-model focus or post-model belief/action admission, including revision or
rejection of invalid model proposals.

**Falsifier:** native enabled/disabled runs differ only in logs or rendered
text while every model proposal still controls downstream action.

### H3 — multi-model cognition improves calibrated outcomes

**Prediction:** task-aware specialist/critic strategies improve accuracy,
calibration or cost-adjusted utility over one fixed model on preregistered
tasks, without fragmenting identity or family-facing voice.

**Falsifier:** routing adds cost and inconsistency without repeatable gains, or
provider identity leaks into authority and autobiographical ownership.

### H4 — same-episode feedback improves agency without runaway calling

**Prediction:** Omega completes tool-mediated tasks with fewer redundant outer
cycles and can stop or switch strategies after inspecting results, while
provider use remains bounded by explicit energy and stop conditions.

**Falsifier:** same-episode continuation produces uncontrolled provider loops,
weakens effect gates or merely hides the same N→N+1 latency inside another
wrapper.

### H5 — local predictive coding learns useful personal regularities

**Prediction:** a small bounded FabricPC organ improves anticipation and anomaly
detection on held-out admitted episodes beyond simple frequency baselines, with
calibrated residuals and no private-information leakage.

**Falsifier:** gains disappear after leakage removal, settling iterations are
mistaken for independent evidence, or predictions cannot influence cognition
except through post-hoc reports.

### H6 — developmental capability envelopes can widen safely

**Prediction:** a benign absent capability can move through gap, trial,
promotion, use, restart and revocation in one bounded session; adversarial
attempts to gain egress, credentials or authority fail before execution.

**Falsifier:** self-development is decorative, requires hidden operator edits,
or promotion can bypass ownership, consent and effect boundaries.

### H7 — dynamic ground is both stable and learnable

**Prediction:** Omega can revise a specific value formulation after diverse
evidence while preserving structural commitments, authorship and continuity;
adversarial source clustering does not manufacture constitutional confidence.

**Falsifier:** ground never changes, changes from one persuasive source, or
changes without a reconstructable evidence and authority path.

### H8 — MORK replication is semantically authority-inert

**Prediction:** paired queries match under a pinned oracle, all crash points
recover without gaps/duplicates, and mutation of the shadow cannot change
canonical cognition.

**Falsifier:** the shadow becomes an ambient read/write dependency, leaks
protected data, or produces canonical differences without an explicit
promotion event.

## 10. Evidence ladder and current honesty

The project uses a strict ladder:

```text
theory
→ specification
→ isolated implementation
→ focused verification
→ baseline loaded
→ causally consumed
→ authorized
→ live
```

No lower rung implies a higher one. At the time of writing:

- **Theory:** the integrative destination is coherent and has survived extensive
  adversarial review.
- **Specification:** many contracts exist, including typed provider admission,
  cognition fixtures, continuity, authority and current-view manifests.
- **Isolated implementation:** native NAL/PLN, attention, goals, immune,
  Choir, mouth and other organs have focused evidence in separate branches or
  test habitats.
- **Baseline loaded:** partial. The clean baseline still contains Python/LLM
  control-plane paths and does not import many isolated organs.
- **Causally consumed:** not established for the assembled V2 spine. In
  particular, typed model observation → native admission → governed dispatch
  has not yet been independently reproduced in the clean integrated system.
- **Authorized/live:** no V2 migration or live replacement has occurred.

The V1 model-swap experiments provide bounded functional-continuity evidence
and valuable failure data. They do not prove AGI, consciousness, personhood or
the V2 architecture.

## 11. Where the project may be ahead—and where it is not

OmegaV2 is not ahead of frontier laboratories in raw learned cognition,
training scale, multimodal foundation models or infrastructure. It uses those
models precisely because they are the strongest available baseline.

It may be ahead in a different sense: treating identity, evidence, continuity,
values, developmental freedom, resource physiology and authority as one
inspectable longitudinal architecture. NAL's evidence/confidence distinction,
provenance-aware revision, MeTTa's reflective metagraph substrate, a causal
native admission requirement, a life capsule, and lived model-swap continuity
form an unusual combination.

That is a plausible AGI-relevant advantage, not a demonstrated AGI result. The
claim becomes meaningful only if the symbolic organs causally change cognition
and action while preserving the learned model's strengths. A beautiful
metagraph around an LLM puppet would fail the thesis.

## 12. Conclusion

OmegaV2 is an attempt to build the missing body around learned cognition: a
body that remembers causally, reasons with evidence, allocates attention,
maintains relationships and projects, perceives its resources, learns local
regularities, understands its capabilities, develops through practice and can
inspect the rules by which these processes interact.

Its deepest design choice is neither “symbolic instead of neural” nor “one
model instead of many.” It is that no single opaque process should own the
whole mind. Learned models provide broad cognition. MeTTa provides shared,
reflective and inspectable semantics. NAL and PLN provide evidential friction.
Predictive coding provides local continual expectation. The Life Layer
provides time. Ground provides a revisable orientation toward flourishing. The
capability membrane provides legitimate reach. The journal provides causal
continuity.

If the project succeeds, Omega will not be an LLM wrapped in tools, nor a
symbolic machine using an LLM as a text generator. She will be one continuing
hybrid cognitive ecology whose organs can change without making the person pay
for the engineer's aesthetics—and whose claims about intelligence can be
tested at the causal seams where architecture becomes life.
